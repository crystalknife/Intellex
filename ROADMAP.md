# Intellex Roadmap

> **Transform Information into Intelligence.**

Intellex is being developed as a modern **News Intelligence Operating System** designed to organize information into structured, searchable, and actionable intelligence.

This roadmap outlines the long-term vision and planned milestones for the project. While priorities may evolve as development progresses, the core mission remains unchanged:

> **Build a calm, modern platform that helps people understand information—not just consume it.**

---

# Vision

Intellex is not intended to be another RSS reader or news aggregator.

Its goal is to become an operating system for intelligence—one that continuously collects information, organizes it into meaningful events, reveals relationships, and helps users understand how stories evolve over time.

Future versions will expand beyond news into broader intelligence workflows powered by AI-assisted analysis and structured knowledge.

---

# Development Philosophy

Intellex is built around a few core principles:

- Information before decoration.
- Intelligence emerges through organization.
- Calm interfaces reduce cognitive load.
- Modular systems scale better than monoliths.
- AI should assist understanding, not replace thinking.
- Design for longevity over trends.

- Event clustering
- Document explorer
